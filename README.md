## Android

### [⬇ Download Ludin for Android](https://github.com/PrranitArora/Ludin-Downloads/raw/main/Ludin.apk)

Version 1.0.1 · Android 5.1 or newer · about 4.6 MB

Open this page **on your Android phone**, then:

1. Tap **Download Ludin for Android** above.
2. If your browser warns that the file might be harmful, tap **Download anyway**.
   Ludin isn't on the Play Store yet, so every phone shows this for it.
3. Open the downloaded file: tap the download notification, or open the
   **Files** app → **Downloads** → **Ludin.apk**.
4. If your phone says it isn't allowed to install apps from this source, tap
   **Settings**, turn on **Allow from this source**, then go back.
5. Tap **Install**. If Google Play Protect says it doesn't recognise the app,
   tap **More details** → **Install anyway**.
6. Open **Ludin** and enter your access code.
7. The first time you arm a WBTB alarm, Ludin asks to send notifications.
   Tap **Allow**: they're the backup alarm in case your phone gets locked.

**Updating:** download the file again and install it over the top. Your
journal and settings are kept.

The iPhone version is installed through **TestFlight**, Apple's app for early
access testing. We'll email you the link as soon as it's ready. When it arrives:

1. Install **[TestFlight](https://apps.apple.com/app/testflight/id899247664)**
   from the App Store (it's free, from Apple).
2. Open the link from our email **on your iPhone** and tap **Accept**, then
   **Install**.
3. Open **Ludin** and enter your access code.
4. When Ludin asks to send notifications, tap **Allow**.

Needs iOS 15 or newer. New versions show up in TestFlight; tap **Update**
there to get them.

## Tips for your first night

- **Leave Ludin open, with the phone plugged in.** Ludin keeps the screen on
  (fully black) all night while a timer or alarm is running, so it uses more
  battery than usual.
- **Don't lock the phone with the power button during a run.** Phones pause
  apps when they're locked. If that happens, a WBTB alarm still rings as a
  notification.
- **iPhone:** if your Ring/Silent switch is set to silent, the backup
  notification vibrates instead of ringing. Ludin's own alarm and spoken cues
  still play.
- **Sensor-Based mode** needs the phone lying on the mattress, screen up.
